# RHL-W3-001 — Company & Industry Research Depth

Status: `IMPLEMENTED / SOL ACCEPTANCE PENDING`

This Wave 3 slice adds independently callable, evidence-gated research depth
for company drivers, unit economics, financial quality, management execution,
capital allocation, market structure, supply-demand cycle, and competitive
mapping. It also adds a Workflow-owned company × industry exposure bridge.

The implementation preserves the accepted architecture: flat canonical Skills,
Workflow-owned composition, no Skill-to-Skill calls, no Agent Runtime, no
Planner, no generic Capability layer, and no Knowledge Schema change. Numeric
results require explicit period-aligned values, units, and source references;
otherwise the result remains partial, unavailable, or qualitative.

## Acceptance boundary

- Company Research remains the existing 19-section Workflow report.
- Industry Research remains the existing eight-module product Workflow; the
  three canonical Industry methods are bounded peers and do not replace it.
- Company × Industry exposure is a Workflow helper, not a new canonical Skill.
- Fixture/offline tests demonstrate deterministic contracts. They do not claim
  authenticated external provider or model availability.

## Runtime result

The canonical catalog now has 17 `IMPLEMENTED`, 7 `PARTIAL`, and 5 `PLANNED`
entries. Runtime registration includes the two new Industry methods and the
previously promoted Company/Financial methods. Dispatch resolves explicit
market-structure, cycle, and competitive queries to one narrow executable
canonical Skill; composite research queries remain Workflow-routed.

The semantic-integrity closure keeps `industry_supply_demand_cycle`,
`management_execution`, and `capital_allocation_review` independently
executable while narrowing their deterministic authority: utilization levels do
not imply direction, qualitative management labels fail closed, and capital
value assessment is action-type and outcome evidence gated.

Readiness wording is intentionally split: `WAVE3_CORE_IMPLEMENTATION_READY` is
`YES`; `WAVE3_AUTHENTICATED_E2E_READY` is `NO / PENDING`; fixture results do not
constitute authenticated production-data validation.

## Deferred

Provider-authenticated live E2E, model-backed semantic quality, and the
remaining partial/planned catalog methods remain outside this bounded slice and
must not be represented as completed by fixture results.
