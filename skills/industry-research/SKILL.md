# Industry Research Skill

This is one Skill containing exactly eight methodology modules: Industry Definition, Market Size & Growth, Supply Demand Analysis, Industry Chain Analysis, Competitive Landscape, Technology Evolution, Company Mapping, and Risk Analysis.

It exposes `industry_research_design`, `industry_module_analysis`, and `industry_cross_module_synthesis`. All reasoning is evidence-only and bounded to the supplied module context. Model identifiers are local proposal keys; the model has no authority over canonical IDs, ChangeSets, Writer actions, paths, or storage. Missing evidence is represented as explicit Research Gaps. Each module has at most one bounded repair attempt.
